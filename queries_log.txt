Natural Language:  What is the total transaction amount recorded in the database?
SQL Query: SELECT SUM(c.amount) AS total_transaction_amount FROM customers c;

Natural Language: How much money was spent by customers from the United States?
SQL Query: SELECT SUM(c.amount) AS total_spent FROM customers c WHERE c.bank_country = 'United States';

Natural Language: Total no of customers
SQL Query: SELECT COUNT(*) FROM customers;

Natural Language: How many transactions occurred after January 1st, 2024?
SQL Query: SELECT COUNT(*) FROM customers WHERE transaction_date > '2024-01-01';
